### The owner's 13 voices (0.14), Ogg Opus, mono, −16 LUFS

Per voice: `adhan_<id>.ogg` (full), `adhan_tk1_<id>.ogg` (the first «الله أكبر الله أكبر», phrase 1),
`adhan_takbir_<id>.ogg` (the four opening takbirs, phrases 1–2), and `adhan_<id>_fajr.ogg` where there is a Fajr adhan.

How the three Fajr adhans were found (names don't say): a normal adhan has 12 sung phrases, a Fajr one 14; and phrases 11–12
carry the hiss of «الصلاة» (energy at 3.5–8 kHz) like the 3rd–6th («أشهد»), where a normal adhan's 11–12 have none.
Also `adhan_tk1_afasy.ogg` and `adhan_tk1_qatami.ogg` (their first «الله أكبر الله أكبر»).
The library states no licence; the recordings belong to their owners. Remove a voice if its owner asks.

## Spoken lines (`spk_*.mp3`)

Generated by the project owner with ElevenLabs (voice "Abdullah – Dramatic Bass", model eleven_v4) from one script,
then cut at the pauses into one file per sentence, loudness-normalised (−16 LUFS), mono 64 kbps MP3.
`spk_exit_*` (before a time ends), `spk_pre_*` (before the adhan), `spk_now_*` (the "spoken alert" at the adhan time),
`spk_iq_*` (iqama), `spk_silent` (phone set to vibrate). The "iqama is near" lines were generated too but are not used yet.
Check that the ElevenLabs plan used allows commercial use before publishing.

> تنبيه (0.18.3): بعض المصادر فيها صورة غلاف مدموجة كمسار فيديو، والجوال يفشل في تشغيلها. كل ملف لازم يكون مسار صوت واحد: `ffprobe -show_entries stream=codec_type` يطلع `audio` بس، وإلا `ffmpeg -i in -map 0:a -c copy out`.
