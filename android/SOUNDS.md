The library states no licence; the recordings belong to their owners. Remove a voice if its owner asks.

## Spoken lines (`spk_*.mp3`)

Generated by the project owner with ElevenLabs (voice "Abdullah – Dramatic Bass", model eleven_v4) from one script,
then cut at the pauses into one file per sentence, loudness-normalised (−16 LUFS), mono 64 kbps MP3.
`spk_exit_*` (before a time ends), `spk_pre_*` (before the adhan), `spk_now_*` (the "spoken alert" at the adhan time),
`spk_iq_*` (iqama), `spk_silent` (phone set to vibrate). The "iqama is near" lines were generated too but are not used yet.
Check that the ElevenLabs plan used allows commercial use before publishing.
